Subdomain Enumeration

Discover hidden subdomains and expand your attack surface with our comprehensive subdomain enumeration tools.

Subfinder

subfinder -d {url} -all -recursive -o subfinder.txt

Subfinder

subfinder -d {url} | alterx | dnsx

Assetfinder

sassetfinder --subs-only {url} > assetfinder.txt

Sublist3r

sublist3r -d {url} -e baidu,yahoo,google,bing,ask,netcraft,virustotal,threatcrowd,crtsh,passivedns -v -o sublist3r.txt

Amass

amass enum -passive -d {url} | cut -d']' -f 2 | awk '{print $1}' | sort -u > amass.txt

Certificate Transparency

curl -s https://crt.sh\?q\=\{url}\&output\=json | jq -r '.[].name_value' | grep -Po '(\w+\.\w+\.\w+)$' >crtsh.txt

Wayback Machine

                        curl -s "http://web.archive.org/cdx/search/cdx?url=*.{url}/*&output=text&fl=original&collapse=urlkey" | sort | sed -e 's_https*://__' -e "s/\/.*//" -e 's/:.*//' -e 's/^www\.//' | sort -u > wayback.txt

Merge & Deduplicate

cat *.txt | sort -u > final.txt

FFUF Subdomain Bruteforce

ffuf -u "https://FUZZ.{url}" -w wordlist.txt -mc 200,301,302

Alterx Enrichment

echo {url} | alterx -enrich | dnsx

Alterx with Wordlist

echo  | alterx -enrich | dnsx
